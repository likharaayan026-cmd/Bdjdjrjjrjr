Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Figo Movies, developed by Aayan Industries, handles information when you use the application.

Last Updated: August 15, 2026
Information Usage Storage Sharing Security Contact

01Information We Collect

Figo Movies may collect information that you provide or that is generated when you use the application.

  • Account information: name, email address and mobile number when you create an account.
  • Authentication information: account credentials are processed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile information: information associated with your user profile, such as your name and mobile number.
  • App content data: movie-related information such as titles, descriptions, genres, posters and video/trailer URLs supplied to the application.
  • Technical information: limited technical information may be processed by third-party services used by the application, depending on their own policies.
We do not ask users to provide sensitive personal information through the Privacy Policy page.

02How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your Figo Movies account.
  • Authenticate users and keep them signed in where supported.
  • Display your profile information inside the app.
  • Provide movie discovery, search, category filtering, trailers and video playback features.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve application functionality.
  • Protect the service against misuse, unauthorized access and technical problems.

03Data Storage & Firebase

Figo Movies uses Firebase services for authentication and application data. Account/profile information may be stored in Firebase services so that the application can provide login and profile functionality.

Movie catalog information is also retrieved from the application's database. Data retention depends on the purpose for which it is stored and the operation of the application.

Third-party infrastructure providers may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

04Data Sharing & Third Parties

Aayan Industries does not sell your personal information as a product. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the app, such as authentication, database, hosting or video-related services.

  • Firebase may process authentication and database information.
  • External video platforms or video hosts may process information when their content is opened or played.
  • Legal authorities may receive information where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Third-party services have their own privacy practices, which may differ from this policy.

05Security

Aayan Industries takes reasonable measures to help protect information used by the application. However, no online service, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should use a strong password and avoid sharing their account credentials with other people.

06Children's Privacy

Figo Movies is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact Aayan Industries so the issue can be reviewed.

07Cookies & Local Storage

The web version of the application may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies where required for functionality, authentication or preferences. Availability and use may vary depending on the platform and browser.

08External Links & Content

Figo Movies may display or open third-party video/content URLs. Aayan Industries does not control the privacy practices, security or content policies of external websites and services.

Users should review the privacy policy of any third-party service they choose to access.

09Your Choices & Account

You may choose whether to provide optional profile information. You can also stop using the application at any time.

If you need assistance regarding your account or personal information, contact Aayan Industries using the contact options below.

10Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, services, legal requirements or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this Privacy Policy page.
